## What worked

- **Setup told me the truth about SES even when it was embarrassing.** The "Cannot send to arbitrary recipients" warning, "Production access: In the SES sandbox", the 200/24h quota with "6 sent in the last 24 hours", and the note that the refusal "reaches the public signup page" — that is exactly what I need to know before I hand this to anyone. And the `Read on every load — never cached` label on that panel is the single most reassuring string in the console.
- **The second sending domain didn't get glossed over.** `www.identithing.com` shows "No SES identity", and the copy goes out of its way to say "Publishing DNS will not help; the identity itself is missing." Most tools would have shown me a pending spinner for a week.
- **Suppression's SES-import warning is written by someone who has been burned.** "Run this BEFORE importing subscribers… the first campaign after a migration mails every one of those addresses — straight into the bounce rate the deliverability halt exists to catch." I'd have made that mistake.
- **Deliverability is one screen I can actually finish.** Three metrics, warn and halt columns, enable checkboxes, and an honest "Without a topic a breach still halts the campaign — you just are not notified." Reputation is disabled with a reason rather than silently broken. This is the whole feature on one page.
- **Audit log states its own integrity properties** — Object-Locked, write-once, "including by whoever wrote them." That's the sentence I want to show an auditor.
- **API & webhooks admits what isn't wired.** The webhooks are HMAC-signed, not key-authenticated, and the page says so unprompted. The outbound-sync section names the deferred work and its issue number (#267) instead of implying HMAC signing exists.
- **Newsletters makes the CAN-SPAM fields required at creation** rather than defaulting them, and says why. Good.
- **Merge tags explains reserved-name precedence** — an imported CSV column called `unsubscribe_url` cannot shadow the real link. That is the failure mode I'd never have thought to check.

## What confused me

- **Setup says "All required steps complete — this organization is ready to send", and on the same page one of my two sending domains has no SES identity at all.** Those two statements cannot both be operationally true. Settings eventually explains it — the checklist's "sending domain" step only means a name is on the org record, not that it verified — but I read the green checkmark first and believed it. That is the wrong default for the one screen a new operator is pointed at.
- **I could not verify a sending identity from anywhere in the console.** That's step two of my job. Setup and Settings both *read* SES state beautifully and neither offers a "create identity" or "re-check verification" action, and there's no DKIM record display to hand to whoever runs DNS. My walk ends at reading, not doing.
- **Organizations is a create form and nothing else.** The nav item is plural. There is no list of existing organizations, no row for the one I'm in, and no switcher on this screen — so after I press `Create organization` I have no surface here that would confirm it worked. I'd have to go to the Audit log's "Cross-org" scope to find out whether my own provisioning landed.
- **Roles & access shows me "14 capabilities" as a bare number with nothing behind it.** I'm the person who decides whether an editor should hold something, and the screen won't tell me what any role actually grants. The role dropdown has a one-line hint for editor; the count has no hint at all. There's also no pending-invite list — after `Send invite` I'd have no evidence the invite exists until the person confirms — and the only action on a member is `Disable`, with no remove and no way to re-scope their organizations after the fact.
- **My API-key leg is hollow before I walk it.** The screen tells me plainly that "no route in this build is authenticated by one" and that "Check a key" is the only thing that authenticates a key today. So I can issue a credential, be shown it once, and the only thing it will ever do is answer a liveness probe. I'd rather the create form said that above the scopes, not in a footnote below them.
- **The Audit log identifies people by raw Cognito sub** (`941804a8-9061-7067-1d1f-b3dc45aad38d`) while Roles & access lists the same humans by email. I cannot read my own audit trail without a lookup table I don't have. And with one entry — a `privacy.export` — I have no evidence the log captures the actions I most care about: org creation, invites, role changes, key issuance.
- **API & webhooks has no permission gate in the nav at all.** I'm the only role that's supposed to issue keys, and the screen that offers the create form is one every role can open. The form renders for everyone and the refusal happens server-side only. That's a UX problem — a control you can fill in and be rejected from is worse than one you can't see.
- **Several screens render a small block of content with an enormous empty region below it, and the sidebar scrolls on its own so the nav list is cut off mid-item.** Deliverability's thresholds table, Data & exports' Erase card and Feeds' empty state all sit in a page that is mostly dead space, with the sidebar ending partway through an entry. It reads like the layout lost the bottom of itself.
- **Campaign report is a blank pane** below the picker until I load something — no placeholder, no description of what I'm about to see. Analytics does the same job with a real explanation above the picker; the report screen should borrow it.
- **Campaigns and Schedules contradict themselves.** A campaign reads `sent` in the status column and `ACTIVE` in the schedule column, with `Pause` and `Archive` rendered enabled on a one-off that already went out. On Schedules, all four rows read ACTIVE including three that have never sent. I cannot tell from either screen what is actually pending.
- **Segments' `Save segment` looks permanently disabled.** The message says "Finish every condition above" while the condition row is visibly incomplete — but a greyed button with a grey hint under a form I thought I'd filled gave me no idea which field was missing.
- **Identity & pools is mostly read-only and says so**, which is fine, but "Magic links are off for this organization, which is a complete and valid configuration — not a missing step" is doing heavy lifting against a screen full of "not configured" fields. I believed the copy. I'd rather the fields themselves read "off by design" than "not configured."
- **The public site's masthead says SUMMIT DAILY while the only newsletter is "Notes from Identithing."** I run this deployment and I cannot find where that wordmark is set — Branding offers logo, colours and presets; none of them is the publisher name my subscribers see first.
- **Usage & cost showed me the word "Loading…" and nothing else for over two seconds on first visit**, and needed a second visit with a six-second wait to resolve. Audit log sat on "Reading the log…" for about three. Neither told me it was slow because it was doing something.
- **DMARC appears nowhere.** Setup says it cannot be shown from there; Settings says it is "still not shown anywhere." Two screens agreeing that a thing is absent is not the same as the thing existing.
