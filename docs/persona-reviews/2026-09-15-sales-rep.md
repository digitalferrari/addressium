## What worked

The Analytics header tells me, up front and in plain words, that every percentage on the screen is a share of sent, that campaign opens and clicks are counted once per subscriber, and that per-link clicks count every click event. That is exactly the sentence I need, because it is the first thing a client asks me — "is that 40% of people or 40% of clicks?" — and I have usually had to guess. Having it written on the screen means I can quote a number without hedging.

Campaigns is honest about what it is not. The "What this table does not show" block says outright that open and click rates live on the report screen, that there is no bulk rates route, and that a recurring series aggregates nothing across its editions. I would much rather be told that than go hunting for a column that was never there. Same for the note that "Audience" is the list and segment a campaign targets, not a recipient count — I would absolutely have read that number as an audience size and put it in a deck.

The Cost estimator is the best screen in here for my purposes. It breaks a send down to SES, KMS, DynamoDB, Lambda and queue transport, gives me $5.29 per send and $0.13 per thousand, separates the $5.80/month that accrues whether or not anyone sends, and then tells me the whole thing is an upper bound captured at list prices in July. When a prospect asks "what does this actually cost to run," I can answer with a real number and be straight about its limits. This is the one screen I would show a client directly.

The dashboard's deliverability tile puts the halt thresholds next to the live rates — bounces 0.0% against a 10% halt, complaints 0.0% against 0.5% — and says "Auto-halt: Armed." That is a good story to tell.

## What confused me

**My job starts on a blank screen.** Campaign report is a picker and then nothing — no placeholder, no sample, no line saying what will appear once I choose. The first time I opened it I genuinely thought it had failed to load. I do not know before I pick whether this screen will give me the per-link click map I came for or just repeat the counters I already saw on Analytics. Put one sentence under the picker describing what loads.

**The headline number is dangerously small.** The 30-day block says "50.0% average open rate." That is one open on two emails sent to two subscribers. If I read that off a screen the morning of a client call, I will say "we're seeing fifty percent open rates" and I will be wrong in a way that costs us the account. The dashboard even warns, in the tile just above, that edition rates are "this edition's own counters, not the 30-day aggregate" — which is precisely the distinction I am going to fumble. Anything under a few hundred sends needs to be labelled as too small to quote.

**There is nowhere to export.** My whole reason for being in here is to get numbers out and into a deck. No export button renders on Analytics, on Campaign report, or on Campaigns. The one screen that does exports is Data & exports, gated on `subscribers:manage` — and even that is a subscriber dump, not report data. So the actual workflow is me retyping percentages into a spreadsheet by hand, which is how a wrong number reaches a client. A "download this report as CSV" on Campaign report would close this.

**Usage & cost did not load the first time.** It showed the heading and the word "Loading…" and stayed there; it took a second visit and a wait to resolve. No spinner, no error, no retry button — just a word. I could not tell whether it was slow or broken, and my instinct was that I was not allowed to see it.

**Why am I looking at the deployment's SES account?** Setup is one of my nine screens and it shows me the sandbox warning "Cannot send to arbitrary recipients," a 24-hour send quota of 200 with 6 used, a max rate of one per second, and per-domain DKIM and MAIL FROM state — all under a green PROD badge. I sell this product. I do not need the send ceiling of the deployment, and I am not sure I should be able to see it. Worse, it shows me `www.identithing.com` sitting there with "No SES identity" and the flat statement that publishing DNS will not fix it. That is a broken thing I cannot act on, cannot explain, and now cannot un-know before a call.

**The same SES block is gated on one screen and not the other.** Settings says its verification-and-quota section is gated on `identity:manage` and that "a role without it sees the Domains list above and not the SES state." But the identical "Sending identity · live from SES" block renders on Setup, which gates on nothing at all. Same account state, two screens, one protected and one not — worth confirming which one is the intended behaviour.

**A read-only screen is offering me credentials.** API & webhooks gates on no capability, so it is in my nav, and what is on it is a "Create key" form with five scope checkboxes including `subscribers:write` and `suppression:write`, plus a "Check a key" box that the copy says is the only thing in the product that authenticates a key and is what sets Last used. Alongside that: the inbound webhook routes, an HMAC-signature description, and a note that rotation and replay protection are deferred under issue #267. I hold `reports:view` and nothing else. Even if every one of those buttons is refused server-side, showing a sales rep a key-issuing form and an internal issue number is the wrong first impression — and the "Check a key" box is a live oracle that tells whoever pastes into it whether a key is valid.

**The lifecycle buttons are the thing I would most want someone to verify.** Campaigns renders [Start] [Pause] [Archive] on every row, and in the capture Pause and Archive appear enabled — on one-offs that have already sent, whose status reads `sent` while the schedule column reads `ACTIVE`. Campaigns is in my nav by design because it gates on `reports:view`, but I hold no `campaigns:schedule`. **These lifecycle controls render on a screen my role can reach; whether they are hidden or disabled for `analyst` is not something this capture can show, and it is the single most important thing to verify.** If they render live to me, the worst day of my career is one stray click on a client's active series. The same question applies to the "＋ New campaign" button on both Campaigns and the Dashboard.

**Small things.** The status/schedule mismatch (`sent` next to `ACTIVE`) makes me unsure which column is authoritative when a client asks if something is still running. And the search box says "Search newsletters, campaigns…" — if it searches campaigns, that is my fastest route to a report, but nothing tells me what it returns.
