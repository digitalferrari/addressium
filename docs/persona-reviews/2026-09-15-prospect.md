## What worked

The page is clean and it loads fast enough, and I like that it doesn't shout at
me. "Choose the newsletters you want in your inbox. No account required — just a
thoughtful email when it matters." That's a nice sentence. No pop-up, no cookie
wall, no "create an account to continue." One box, one button, done — the signup
is right there on the card so I don't have to click through to a second page to
find out what's being asked of me. And it says I can unsubscribe at any time down
at the bottom, which I appreciate seeing before I hand anything over rather than
after.

## What confused me

Whose site am I on? The logo at the top says **SUMMIT DAILY** in big serif
letters, but the one thing I can actually sign up for is called "The Dispatch"
and underneath it says "Notes from Identithing." Summit Daily, The Dispatch,
Identithing — that's three names on one small page and I can't tell which of them
is going to appear in my inbox, or who any of them are. There's no "about," no
byline, no person's name.

And what *is* it about? "Ideas worth opening" is a nice phrase but it isn't a
subject. "Notes from Identithing" is one line and it tells me nothing. Ideas
about what — tech, local news, cooking? How often does it come, daily or when the
mood strikes? There's no past issue to look at, no archive link, no sample, no
"we send on Tuesdays." I'm being asked to give my email address to a stranger and
take on trust that it's worth it, with no way to check first. For something I'd
otherwise probably have signed up for, that's where I stall.

The address bar bothers me a bit too. It's a long string of letters on
`cloudfront.net` — not `summitdaily.com`, not `identithing.com`. It looks like a
staging link someone sent me by mistake. If a friend forwarded me this I'd wonder
if it were a phishing page.

Then at the very bottom: "Powered by addressium." A fourth name. I don't know
what addressium is, and I'm not sure why the publisher wants me to. It reads like
I'm on a tool's page rather than a magazine's.

The thing I'd most want fixed: after I press Subscribe, what happens? Nothing on
this page tells me. So a version of me who pressed the button would be sitting
there looking at the screen, waiting for something — not knowing whether I'm
done, or whether something's on its way to my inbox that I'm supposed to act on.
Nothing on the page tells me either way, and nothing tells me what such a message
would be called or who it would be from, so I wouldn't know what to look for. If
nothing showed up I'd assume the signup failed and I'd never go back.

One more, on the "Subscribe to all" page: the header above it still says "No
account required — just a thoughtful email when it matters," but the page itself
is titled "Choose your inbox" with a checkbox that starts *unchecked* and a
counter reading "1 available." So the menu I'm being invited to choose from has
one item on it, and if I type my address and hit Subscribe without noticing the
box, I don't know what I've just signed up for. "Subscribe to all" in the menu and
"pick the ones you'd like" on the page are telling me two different things.
